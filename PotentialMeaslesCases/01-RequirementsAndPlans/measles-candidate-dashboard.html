How to Read This

Each candidate below has a potential-measles signal drawn from TRN, ADT, CCD, or lab data, and no matching reportable-condition report on file. Concern level reflects how many independent signals line up — e.g., a measles-specific lab order plus a fever-and-rash encounter is higher concern than a non-specific viral rash alone. Click a name to open that person's case letter, which includes the methodology, the specific signals found, and First / Last / DOB / Address / SOURCE|MRN for easy lookup.
