Reading This Responsibly

  • This reflects only the sources contributing to PDR — roughly 200 hospitals and 1,000 lab sources, plus thousands of non-hospital practices. It is not statewide surveillance, and absence of a candidate here does not mean absence of measles activity.
  • No automated already-reported exclusion yet. The "Report on File?" column reflects manual review for this fictitious example, not a system check. A future feed from the state's upstate and downstate reportable-condition systems could let us remove already-reported cases automatically before a reviewer sees them — until then, a candidate here may already be known to public health.
  • Concern level and signal weighting are first-pass heuristics (see the methodology), not a validated clinical score.
  • This is a candidate list for human review — nothing here should be forwarded, reported, or acted on without clinical and public-health confirmation.
